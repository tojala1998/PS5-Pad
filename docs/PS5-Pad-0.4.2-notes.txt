PS5 Pad 0.4.2 (build 8)

Fixes a reconnection edge case found during review of 0.4.1: if Initialize
Controller or another connection prompt was left open while the app became
inactive, the old prompt prevented reconnecting when the app returned.

On reactivation the app dismisses its stale connection/Kodi prompts, reconnects
when a connection is wanted, and offers a fresh Initialize Controller prompt.
It preserves Find your PS5 and any address being entered there. Reconnection
is guarded by the app's active state.

Includes the 0.4.1 permission-first scan, faster address ordering/concurrency,
initialization prompt and reminder. Same identity: com.uhnmeyker.ps5pad.
No settings are seeded, migrated or reset. Controller service remains 2.1.
System-wide contextual keyboard support is not included yet.

Validation: existing protocol, input queue, UIKit selector and discovery checks;
ARM64 build with warnings treated as errors and unsigned archive validation.
The resume scenario needs on-device confirmation after this update is installed.
